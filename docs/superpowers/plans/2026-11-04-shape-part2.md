# Shape thing, part 2: the paint layers (plan)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-shape-thing-design.md`, section "Part 2: the paint layers" (read the whole
spec first). Part 1 (PR #76, merged) gave the `shape` thing, its `look.shape` card with only the base `shape.body`
layer, and the plain-white paths pipeline (`bpe-gfx/src/shaders/paths.wgsl`, `PathInstance`, `Frame::push_path`).

Part 2 delivers the layers a shape's Look can stack over its plain white Body: **Colour and gradient**, **Outline**,
**Glow**, **Blend** and **Shader**, drawn on the GPU by a layered walk in the paths pipeline, with in-shape Shader
layers built into the same in-ball programs the balls use, and the editor's Look card listing, adding and editing
them. No masks, reactions or group layers for shapes (not in the spec).

Model: every step on Opus at high effort. One Opus review at the end (task 9), then the PR. No demo renders or
screenshots unless the owner asks.

## Decisions (made while planning; tell the owner in the PR)

1. **Blend is whole-shape, wherever it sits in the list.** It works like the balls' Blend layer with nothing above
   it: the finished shape is mixed with what is behind it (through `ThingDraw::blend`). There is no two-part split
   for layers above it. Its settings are the balls' `BlendLayer` (Blend, Blend amount, Blend with each other).
2. **Colour and gradient** covers exactly what the Body covers (the fill, or the band of a hollow shape). One colour,
   or several running:
   - **Across**: along a Direction in degrees (90 = top to bottom, as balls' Direction), from the shape's first
     point along that direction to its last, so the end colours sit on the shape's own edges. It turns with the shape.
   - **From the middle**: a filled shape runs from its middle (0) to its furthest point (1). A hollow shape runs
     **across its band** from the inner edge to the outer edge (a middle-to-furthest run would show only the last
     colour on a thin band).
   - **Along the edge**: once round the outline, starting at the top (point 0 of the outline: the top corner of an
     even polygon (the left of two when its top is flat), the top of a circle, the top-left corner of a
     rectangle) and going clockwise.
   - **Flow** (several colours only, colours per second, span −2..2, keyframe): the colours move along the run and
     wrap round. 0 keeps them still (and the run clamps, so the far end shows the last colour exactly).
3. **Outline** is a line along every edge: the outline of a filled shape, both edges of a hollow band. Width (px),
   Sits (Inside / Centre / Outside, relative to the shape's area: "inside" a band means towards the band's middle),
   Colour from (Same as body = the colour of the layers under it at that point, a little darker (×0.6, as balls do),
   or Own), Colour (gradient), Gradient (Across / From the middle / Along the edge / **Across the line**: from its
   inner side to its outer side), Direction, Flow, Opacity, Blend.
4. **Glow** is soft light outside every edge (also into the hole of a hollow shape): Amount (0..2), Size (px, how
   far it reaches; the light falls off as `exp(-(d/size)²)` like the balls' halo), Colour from (Same as body = the
   colour under it), Colour, Gradient (Across / From the middle / Along the edge / **Outward**: from the edge out to
   its Size), Direction, Flow, Opacity, Blend.
5. **Shader** is the balls' Shader layer (same effects, own code, Speed, Scale, Opacity, Blend), run inside the
   shape with the layers under it as its picture. Its uv covers the shape's square (twice its reach across, its
   middle at 0.5), turning with the shape or, with **Stays with: Screen**, upright. The Stays with choices read
   "Shape" / "Screen" (stored "shape"/"screen"; `Space::from_value` reads "shape" as its default, Ball, which is the
   turning one).
6. A new Shape still starts with the Body alone (white): adding a Colour and gradient layer is how it gets colour.
   The new layer's default colour is a clear blue (`Color::rgb(64, 156, 255)`) so it shows at once on white.
7. Masks, reactions and "only some balls" don't apply to shape layers (`maskable` false, `per_ball` false).
8. Copy look / Paste look work between shapes (and between balls), never across: Paste look is offered when every
   copied layer's kind belongs to the target card.

## Task 2: the paths pipeline walks a shape's records (`bpe-gfx`, WGSL)

Files: `frame.rs`, `lib.rs` (`PATH_ATTRIBUTES`, `layers_source`), `shaders/paths.wgsl`, new
`shaders/shape_layers.wgsl` (helpers), new `shaders/shape_walk.wgsl` (the walk template).

1. `PathInstance` gains `pub layers: [u32; 4]`: first record in `Frame::look_layers`, how many (0 = plain, drawn
   in `color` exactly as part 1), the in-shape program (`PathInstance::NO_PROGRAM = u32::MAX` for none; an index
   into `Frame::programs`; read on the CPU only), spare. `c.z` becomes "how far its layers reach past its outline,
   in world pixels" (an outline outside, a glow) and `c.w` "how far its outline reaches from the middle, in shape
   units, for From the middle" (set = `c.y`). `reach()` adds `c.z`. `push_path` returns the new instance's index and
   sets `layers = [0, 0, NO_PROGRAM, 0]`, `c.z = 0`, `c.w = reach`. `PATH_ATTRIBUTES` gains location 4 `Uint32x4`.
   Doc the shape records table on `LayerGpu` (below) and add `LayerGpu::SHAPE_PAINT = 10`.
2. Shape records (bottom first, the Body's first), read only by the shape walk:

   | code | layer | `amount` | `p0` | `p1` | flag 1 |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | 0 | body | opacity | - | - | - |
   | 10 | colour and gradient | opacity | runs (0 across, 1 middle, 2 edge) | dir xy, lo, hi | - |
   | 2 | outline | opacity | width px, sits (0 in, 1 centre, 2 out), runs (3 across the line) | dir xy, lo, hi | colour from the layers under it |
   | 4 | glow | opacity, strength | size px, -, runs (3 outward) | dir xy, lo, hi | colour from the layers under it |
   | 6 | shader | opacity | speed, scale, space (0 shape, 1 screen), effect slot | - | - |

   `colour` is the record's own linear colour (its gradient's middle); `paint` its gradient (`row` -1 for one
   colour) with `scroll` = Flow and `repeat` 1 when Flow is not 0. `lo`/`hi` are where the run's Across starts and
   ends along `dir`, in shape units. `head.y` is the blend against the layers under it.
3. `paths.wgsl`: `PathIn` gains `@location(4) layers: vec4<u32>`, `PathOut` `@location(5) @interpolate(flat)
   layers`. `vs_path`'s reach adds `s.c.z`. `shade_path`: when `in.layers.y == 0u` exactly today's code; otherwise
   set `layered_shape` (below), `cover = path_cover(in)`, `w = walk_shape(in.world, in.layers.x, in.layers.y)`,
   `out = cover * w.inner + (1 - cover) * w.outer`, discard when it is all but zero. `fs_path_object` unchanged.
4. `shape_layers.wgsl` (appended by `layers_source` right after `layers.wgsl`, before the walks):
   - `struct LayeredShape { a, b, c: vec4<f32>, layers: vec4<u32> }` and `var<private> layered_shape`.
   - `struct ShapeAt { q: vec2<f32>, sd: f32, edge: f32, along: f32 }` and `fn shape_at(world) -> ShapeAt`: `q` the
     point in shape units; `sd` the signed distance to the outline's centre line in world px (negative inside,
     even-odd); `edge` the distance to the edge of the shape's area in world px, negative inside it (`sd` filled,
     `abs(sd) - half` hollow); `along` 0..1 round the outline from point 0 clockwise (circle: `fract(atan2(q.x,
     -q.y) / TAU)`; points: arc length at the nearest point over the perimeter, segments from point k to k+1).
   - `fn shape_cover_at(world) -> f32`: `path_cover`'s maths at any point (used by `sample`).
   - `fn shape_run(l: LayerGpu, runs: u32, s: ShapeAt, outward: f32) -> f32`: 0 across `(dot(q, p1.xy) - p1.z) /
     max(p1.w - p1.z, 1e-6)`, 1 middle (`length(q) / c.w` filled; `(sd + half) / (2 half)` hollow), 2 `along`,
     3 `outward`; clamped by `paint_along`'s wrap.
   - `fn shape_colour(l, runs, s, outward) -> vec3<f32>`: `l.colour.rgb` when `l.paint.row < 0`, else
     `paint_along(l.paint, shape_run(..), g.view.w).rgb`.
5. `shape_walk.wgsl`, a template like `layers_walk.wgsl` (names `SHAPE_WALK`, `//SHAPE_SHADER_CASE`):
   `fn SHAPE_WALK(world: vec2<f32>, first: u32, count_in: u32) -> Walked`. `aa = g.view.z` (world px per output
   pixel). Over `min(count, MAX_LAYERS)` records, with `layer_over` and `band` from layers.wgsl:
   - 0: `c = l.colour.rgb`, `a_in = opacity`.
   - 10: `c = shape_colour(l, u32(p0.x), s, 0)`, `a_in = opacity`.
   - 2: `w = max(p0.x, 0)`; lo/hi = (-w, 0) inside, (-w/2, w/2) centre, (0, w) outside over `s.edge`;
     `c` = flag ? (colour under it as if inside: `inner.rgb / inner.a`, white when nothing) × 0.6 :
     `shape_colour(l, u32(p0.z), s, clamp((edge - lo) / (hi - lo), 0, 1))`; `a_in = band(edge, lo, hi, aa, true) *
     opacity`, `a_out = band(edge, lo, hi, aa, false) * opacity`.
   - 4: `gd = max(edge, 0) / max(p0.x, 1)`; `c` = flag ? colour under it (as above, not darkened) :
     `shape_colour(l, u32(p0.z), s, clamp(gd, 0, 1))`; `a_out = max(amount.y, 0) * exp(-gd²) * opacity`, faded to 0
     over the last fifth of the quad (`smoothstep(0.8, 1.0, dist_from_centre_px / quad_reach_px)` with the quad
     reach as `vs_path` works it out), light added into `outer` as the balls' glow is.
   - `//SHAPE_SHADER_CASE`.
   Compositing as the ball walk: glow adds light to `outer` (mixed by its blend with what is there); the rest go
   through `layer_over` into `inner` (a_in) and `outer` (a_out).
6. `layers_source(effects)`: after `layers.wgsl`, push `shape_layers.wgsl`; `None` pastes `walk_shape` without the
   shader case; `Some` pastes `walk_shape_plain` (no shader case, for `sample`) and `walk_shape` (with
   `shaders/shape_shader_case.wgsl`).
7. Tests (`paths_tests.rs`, software GPU via `pictures::render_sized`; skip when no adapter as the others do):
   a filled 400 × 200 rectangle with body + an Across red→blue record (dir (1,0), lo -1, hi 1) is red at its left
   edge, blue at its right, purple in the middle; Along the edge on a hollow circle: top-right quarter and
   bottom-left quarter differ; an Outside outline on a filled square: pixel 3 px outside is the outline colour, the
   centre is the paint colour; a Same-as-body outline over a red paint is dark red; a glow on a filled circle lights
   a pixel 10 px outside, a pixel 3 glow sizes out is dark; a hollow band with layers covers the same pixels as the
   plain band (band middle bright, hole dark); a body-only record list draws the same white as part 1's plain path.
